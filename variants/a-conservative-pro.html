Long-term hires

Permanent recruitment

Every level from commis chefs and waiters to head chefs and general managers. Permanent recruitment focuses on culture and values fit, because a strong CV lasts an interview and a good fit lasts years.

We brief every candidate on your menu, your covers and your expectations before they walk through the door, so interviews start at substance rather than small talk.

Same-week starts

Temporary staffing

Peaks, events, sickness and maternity cover, with same-week starts from a pool of vetted chefs, waiters, baristas and kitchen porters who arrive ready to work your section, your till or your pass.

Every temp is interviewed, reference checked and rated after each assignment. Rates are agreed upfront and one point of contact handles bookings, swaps and invoices.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a hospitality recruitment agency in London do?
A hospitality recruitment agency in London recruits staff on behalf of hospitality businesses. Hugo sources, screens and shortlists candidates for restaurants, hotels, caterers, cafes and private venues, then supports both sides through to the start date. Fees are billed to the employer, so candidates register free and businesses pay only for successful placements.
How quickly can you fill a hospitality vacancy?
Shortlists typically arrive within days of a brief, because we match from thousands of registered candidates rather than starting from scratch. We recently placed both an Assistant General Manager and a Head Chef for one restaurant within a week of the first conversation.
Do you place permanent or temporary staff?
Both. Permanent recruitment covers long-term hires at every level, from waiters and commis chefs to general managers and head chefs, with a focus on culture and values fit. Temporary staffing covers peaks, events and absence, with same-week starts from a pool of vetted chefs, waiters and porters.
Which sectors does Hugo cover?
Hugo staffs six sectors: restaurants, hotel operations, event and catering, bakery and cafe, fine dining and private clubs with private chefs, and health care and education. Each sector has its own specialist consultant, so briefs are matched by someone who knows the roles, the pace and the compliance requirements.
Can you place chefs in schools and care homes?
Yes. Our health care and education desk places chefs and kitchen teams in schools, nurseries and care homes. These settings carry dietary, allergen and regulatory requirements, so candidates are screened for compliance experience, including allergen controls, nutrition planning and safeguarding awareness, before any introduction is made.
