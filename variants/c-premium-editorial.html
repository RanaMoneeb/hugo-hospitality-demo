Permanent recruitment

Every level from commis chefs and waiters to head chefs and general managers — focused on culture and values fit, because a strong CV lasts an interview and a good fit lasts years.

Temporary staffing

Peaks, events, sickness and maternity cover, from a pool of vetted chefs, waiters, baristas and kitchen porters who arrive ready to work your section, your till or your pass.

Every temp is interviewed, reference checked and rated after each assignment. Rates are agreed upfront and one point of contact handles bookings, swaps and invoices.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does a hospitality recruitment agency in London do?
A hospitality recruitment agency in London recruits staff on behalf of hospitality businesses. Hugo sources, screens and shortlists candidates for restaurants, hotels, caterers, cafes and private venues, then supports both sides through to the start date. Fees are billed to the employer, so candidates register free and businesses pay only for successful placements.
How quickly can you fill a hospitality vacancy?
Shortlists typically arrive within days of a brief, because we match from thousands of registered candidates rather than starting from scratch. We recently placed both an Assistant General Manager and a Head Chef for one restaurant within a week of the first conversation.
Do you place permanent or temporary staff?
Both. Permanent recruitment covers long-term hires at every level, with a focus on culture and values fit. Temporary staffing covers peaks, events and absence, with same-week starts from a pool of vetted chefs, waiters and porters.
Which sectors does Hugo cover?
Hugo staffs six sectors: restaurants, hotel operations, event and catering, bakery and cafe, fine dining and private clubs with private chefs, and health care and education. Each sector has its own specialist consultant.
Can you place chefs in schools and care homes?
Yes. Our health care and education desk places chefs and kitchen teams in schools, nurseries and care homes, with candidates screened for allergen controls, nutrition planning and safeguarding awareness before any introduction.
